Important limits for legal, food, animal, emergency, and map features.

Food, Farm Sales, and Legal Compliance

The Homesteading Compass cannot determine whether a product, animal, service, event, or listing is legal in your city, county, state, or country. Verify cottage food, egg, dairy, meat, honey, nursery, tax, market, animal transport, zoning, and business rules with official sources before selling or buying.

Livestock and Veterinary Care

Animal notes, logs, reminders, and educational content are not veterinary advice. For illness, injury, medication, withdrawal times, pregnancy concerns, disease risk, or animal welfare questions, contact a licensed veterinarian or appropriate extension/professional resource.

Medical, Emergency, and Safety Decisions

Emergency plans, weather notes, first-aid reminders, and preparedness content are planning tools only. In an emergency, contact local emergency services and follow official alerts, evacuation orders, and professional instructions.

Maps and Location

Map pins, distances, address lookups, and location privacy tools are estimates. Do not rely on the app for emergency navigation, property boundaries, trespass decisions, delivery precision, or exact legal locations.

User-Posted Content

Listings, events, messages, map pins, certifications, permits, claims, and business details are posted by users. The Homesteading Compass does not automatically inspect, certify, guarantee, or endorse user-posted content.
