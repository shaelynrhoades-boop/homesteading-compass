Account and Data

Account and Data Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your The Homesteading Compass account, or ask us to delete specific data while keeping your account.

Request Deletion in the App

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Open Settings, then Account Safety.
  3. Select Request Account Deletion and confirm.

The app submits the request and signs you out. The optional feedback questions are not required.

Request Deletion Without the App

Email from the address used for your account and write “Delete my Homesteading Compass account.” We may ask for limited additional information to verify that you control the account.

Email an Account Deletion Request

If the button does not open your email app, write to support@thehomesteadingcompass.com.

Delete Specific Data and Keep Your Account

You can remove many records, listings, posts, replies, photos, saved items, sharing choices, and notification settings inside the app. You may also ask us to delete specific data without deleting your account.

  1. Email us from the address used for your account.
  2. Describe the specific records or data you want deleted.
  3. We will verify the request, delete or de-identify the requested data when permitted, and confirm completion by email.

Email a Data Deletion Request

Verified requests are processed within 30 days. Data may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or moderation records, or longer when reasonably required for fraud prevention, safety, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

What Is Deleted

After verification, we permanently delete the sign-in account and associated data that we are not legally required to retain. This includes private app records, profile data, messages, photos and uploaded files, public posts and replies, marketplace and Outpost listings, saved items, notification tokens, and other user content associated with the account. Shared or public records may be deleted or de-identified so they no longer identify you.

Timing and Limited Retention

We process verified requests within 30 days and send confirmation to the account email when deletion is complete. Limited information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or moderation records, or longer when reasonably required for fraud prevention, safety, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. It will not remain available as an active account.

Account deletion is permanent. Export or copy records you need before submitting the request. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.
