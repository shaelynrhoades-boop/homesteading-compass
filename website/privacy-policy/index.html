Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 17, 2026

Who This Policy Covers

This policy describes how The Homesteading Compass handles information in its mobile app, website, support channels, and related community and marketplace features. The Homesteading Compass is the name of the app and service covered by this policy.

Information You Provide

We may collect your name, email address, account identifier, profile and homestead details, contact information you choose to publish, support requests, notification choices, and content you enter. User content can include animal, garden, harvest, pantry, finance, farm, recipe, Notebook, chore, supply, emergency-plan, marketplace, map, class, event, question, reply, photo, message, and report information.

Location, Photos, Notifications, and Diagnostics

With your permission, the app may access location for local weather, address lookup, maps, approximate distance, and nearby activity. It may access the camera or photo library when you choose a photo. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token and your preferences. We may record technical information such as app area, platform, error message, severity, and time when an error occurs. The app does not request microphone access.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts; save, sync, and restore records; provide weather, map, community, marketplace, messaging, sharing, notification, support, moderation, and photo-assistance features; prevent abuse; diagnose failures; enforce our rules; and improve service reliability. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party behavioral advertising.

Private, Shared, and Public Information

Personal logs, account settings, Notebook content, contacts, chores, and emergency information are intended to remain private unless you use a sharing or public-posting feature. Information shared with an invited participant is visible according to the selected permission. Public profiles, posts, replies, listings, seller information, map pins, event or class details, photos, city/state, and contact methods you deliberately publish can be seen by other users. Avoid publishing a precise home address or information you do not want shared.

Service Providers and Disclosures

We use service providers to operate the service, including Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions; Expo for app delivery, updates, and push notifications; Mapbox for maps and address lookup; Tomorrow.io for weather and location lookup; OpenAI for optional photo-assistance features; and Apple or Google for app distribution and any future store-billed purchases. These providers receive information needed to perform their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, investigate abuse, enforce our agreements, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

AI-Assisted Photo Features

When you deliberately use a photo-assistance feature, the selected image and related prompt may be sent through our protected server function to OpenAI to suggest draft details. Review every suggestion before saving or publishing it. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or identifying images that are unnecessary for the feature.

Payments

Sellers may display accepted payment methods, but The Homesteading Compass does not ask them to store payment handles or bank details in those fields and does not process or guarantee marketplace payments. If digital subscriptions are offered later, Apple or Google will process purchases; we will not receive full payment-card details.

Retention

We keep account information and saved content while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Deleted content may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or moderation records until routine deletion cycles finish. We may retain limited records when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, safety, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or enforcement. Public content removed through account deletion will be deleted or de-identified unless retention is legally required.

Your Controls and Requests

You can edit or remove many records, listings, posts, photos, sharing permissions, public contact choices, and notification settings inside the app. You may request access, correction, export help, or deletion by using Settings > Account Safety or by emailing support@thehomesteadingcompass.com. Privacy rights vary by location, and we will honor applicable rights after reasonably verifying the request.

Account Deletion

An account-deletion request starts permanent deletion of the account and associated data that we are not legally required to retain, including public user content. Requests are processed within 30 days and completion is confirmed by email. The account may remain active until processing is complete. You can start a request in the app or on the Account Deletion page.

Security and Processing Locations

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including authenticated access and database access policies. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. Marketplace selling, farmhand listings, and other transactions may require users to be 18 or have appropriate adult involvement under local law.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to support@thehomesteadingcompass.com.
