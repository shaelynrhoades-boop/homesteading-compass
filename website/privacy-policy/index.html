Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

Homesteading Compass respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Homesteading Compass may collect or store information that you choose to enter, including account details, homestead profile information, animal records, garden records, chores, notes, emergency plans, supply lists, photos, marketplace listings, messages, reports, seller information, and app support requests.

Account Information

If you create an account or sign in, we may store account-related information such as your email address, user ID, profile details, and authentication information needed to operate your account.

How Information Is Used

We use information to provide app features, save your records, sync data where available, show marketplace or community listings, manage seller profiles, support messaging/reporting features, provide weather/map tools, respond to support requests, improve app reliability, and maintain app safety.

Private Records

Personal homestead records such as animal logs, garden logs, chores, emergency plans, notes, and supply lists are intended to remain private unless you choose to share or publish information through a public feature.

Public Features

Some features may allow you to post or share information with other users. This may include Trading Post listings, Farm Stand listings, Porch Talk posts, Porch Light Map pins, profile information, listing photos, pickup city/state, and contact details you choose to provide.

Location Information

Homesteading Compass may use location information for weather, maps, marketplace listings, and Porch Light Map features. You control what location details you choose to enter or publish. Public map and listing features may show approximate or selected location details depending on your settings.

Photos and Uploaded Content

If you upload photos for animal profiles, listings, support messages, or other app features, those images may be stored so the feature can work properly. Photos attached to public listings or public posts may be visible to other users.

Messages, Reports, and Community Safety

If you send messages, post community content, submit reports, or contact support, that content may be stored and reviewed as needed to operate the app, respond to issues, enforce rules, and protect users.

Payments and Subscriptions

If paid subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads are offered, payment processing is handled by the app store or applicable third-party provider. Homesteading Compass does not directly collect full payment card information.

Third-Party Services

Homesteading Compass may use third-party services to operate the app, including services for authentication, database storage, file storage, maps, weather, app updates, analytics, crash/error reporting, and app store purchases.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to help protect stored information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can edit many records inside the app, remove listings or posts where available, adjust privacy settings, and choose what information to share publicly.

Account Deletion and Data Requests

You may request help with account deletion, data access, or data removal by contacting support at the email address below.

Children

Homesteading Compass is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need help with your account, contact: support@thehomesteadingcompass.com
