Marketplace Rules

Rules for safer, clearer Trading Post listings.

Allowed Listings

  • Legal homestead goods, farm products, animals, supplies, equipment, handmade items, and services that can be safely sold in your area.
  • Listings with honest details, clear photos, fair descriptions, and accurate pickup or delivery expectations.
  • Animals with health, temperament, age, sex, breed, registration, and known defects disclosed when relevant.
  • Listings drafted from Farm Stand, livestock, Workshop, or other app records, as long as the public listing details are accurate.

Prohibited Listings

  • Illegal goods, stolen property, recalled products, unsafe food, or anything prohibited by local, state, or federal law.
  • Uninspected meat sold as inspected meat, false organic claims, false registration claims, or false certifications.
  • Animals with hidden illness, dangerous temperament, known defects, or transport restrictions that are not disclosed.
  • Listings that encourage unsafe handling, illegal slaughter/resale, harassment, scams, or payment fraud.

Seller Responsibilities

  • Verify your local cottage food, meat, egg, dairy, honey, animal transport, and market rules before posting.
  • Use accurate labels, weights, dates, prices, photos, and records.
  • Check transferred or auto-filled listing details before publishing.
  • Remove, pause, or mark listings sold when they are no longer available.
  • Respond honestly to buyer questions and do not pressure buyers to pay before they can verify the listing.

Buyer Responsibilities

  • Inspect animals and items before paying when possible.
  • Do not send deposits to unknown sellers without understanding the risk.
  • Ask for records, labels, registration papers, health paperwork, or butcher details when they matter.
  • Follow your local laws for pickup, transport, resale, and food handling.

Moderation

  • The Homesteading Compass may remove, pause, or review listings that appear unsafe, misleading, illegal, or reported by users.
  • Reports do not guarantee immediate action, but they help keep the marketplace safer.
  • Repeated unsafe, dishonest, or abusive behavior may lead to loss of marketplace access.

Not a Party to the Sale

  • The Homesteading Compass helps buyers and sellers connect, but the actual sale is between the buyer and seller.
  • The app does not inspect items, certify sellers, guarantee animal health, verify legal compliance, or process payment for Trading Post listings.
