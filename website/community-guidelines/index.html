Community Guidelines

Expectations for Porch Talk, Porch Light, Outpost, Trading Post, and messages.

Be Neighborly

  • Treat other users with respect, especially when discussing animals, food, land, weather, politics, pricing, or local customs.
  • Do not harass, threaten, shame, stalk, or pressure another user.
  • Keep disagreements practical and focused on safety, facts, and experience.

Post Useful Local Information

  • Use clear titles, dates, towns, prices, contact expectations, and accurate categories.
  • Mark events, listings, and offers unavailable when they are over, sold, paused, or no longer accurate.
  • Do not spam the same event, business, listing, or service repeatedly.

Keep Public Spaces Safe

  • Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, photos, or personal details for someone else without permission.
  • Do not post graphic, unsafe, illegal, fraudulent, or intentionally misleading content.
  • Use report buttons when something looks unsafe, abusive, dishonest, or illegal.

Trade and Meet Carefully

  • Meet in safe public or appropriate farm locations, tell someone where you are going, and trust your judgment.
  • Inspect animals, goods, labels, and paperwork before paying when possible.
  • Follow local rules for food, livestock, transport, processing, permits, and sales.
